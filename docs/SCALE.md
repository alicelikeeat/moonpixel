# Scale (v3.0)

Everything in the world is drawn to one scale, as in life.

| | |
|---|---|
| One tile | 2 m |
| One storey | 2 tiles = 4 m |
| Speeds | tiles a second × 7.2 = km/h (a street's 7 tiles/s is 50 km/h) |

## People

| | real | tiles |
|---|---|---|
| A grown-up | 1.75 m | 0.875 (3D figure; eyes at 0.82) |
| A child | about 1.2 m | 0.6 |

On the 2D map people are drawn at 78 % of the sprite, still a little larger than life so they can be seen and tapped.
